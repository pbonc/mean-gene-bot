# Meatwad Class Guide (tankadelphia)

## Overview
Meatwad is a special transformation-based class exclusive to **tankadelphia**. Unlike other classes with multiple skills, Meatwad has one powerful skill: **!transform**.

## Class Stats
- **Tier**: 1 (Special)
- **Base HP**: 20 (Ascended class)
- **Battle Skill**: !transform
- **Stream Skill**: None

## Core Mechanic: Transformations

### How It Works
1. Use **!transform** during battle to randomly shift into one of 30 different forms
2. Forms are **level-gated** (you can only roll forms at or below your current level)
3. Each form has unique passive effects that apply automatically
4. You remain in that form until you transform again
5. Transformation happens FIRST in turn order (before all other actions)
6. Damage and healing work normally - forms add special effects on top

### Transformation Command
```
!transform
```
- Can only be used during battle (action window)
- One use per turn
- Form selected randomly based on rarity weights **within your level gates**
- Form persists across turns until you transform again

## Strategic Guide

### When to Use Each Type

**Offensive Forms** (Monster, Lincoln, Humanoid, Hammer, Slim Jim)
- Use when you need to maximize damage output
- Best against high-HP enemies
- Pairs well with crit-focused strategies

**Defensive Forms** (Igloo, Humanoid 2, Bricks, Wall, Humanoid 3)
- Use when taking heavy damage
- Best against multiple attackers or hard-hitting enemies
- Humanoid 3 provides both offense and defense

**Evasion Forms** (Wesley Snipes, Paper)
- Use as alternative to defense
- Best against predictable damage patterns
- High risk but can completely negate damage

**Regeneration Forms** (Hot Dog, Pregnant, Flower, Cow, Honey Bear)
- Use for sustain in long battles
- Hot Dog and Flower heal party, others heal self
- Pairs well with defensive forms

**AoE/DoT Forms** (Tornado, Spider)
- Use against multiple enemies
- Tornado for burst, Spider for sustained damage
- Excellent for clearing weak monsters

**Reflect/Counter Forms** (Chili, Nacho, Middle Finger)
- Use to punish attackers
- Middle Finger can get killing blows
- Counter damage counts toward your stats

**Synergy Forms** (Bridge 2.0, Famous Phrase, Bridge)
- Use with full party for maximum effect
- Bridge 2.0 and Famous Phrase scale with party size
- Bridge protects entire party

**Special Forms**
- **Master Shake**: Gambling/chaos - fun but unreliable
- **Office Building**: Emergency HP when in danger
- **Satellite Dish**: Crit fishing
- **Number 5**: Solid all-around choice

### Transformation Strategy Tips

1. **Early Battle**: Transform on first turn to see what you get
2. **Adapt**: If you get a bad form for the situation, transform again next turn
3. **Commit**: Good forms are worth keeping for multiple turns
4. **Party Awareness**: Synergy forms shine with full party
5. **Enemy Count**: Use AoE/DoT forms against packs, single-target against bosses
6. **HP Tracking**: Use defensive/regen forms when low, offensive when healthy

### Form Tier List by Situation

**Best Overall**: Mythical forms (all excellent), Humanoid 3, Tornado
**Best Offense**: Monster, Lincoln, Bridge 2.0, Satellite Dish
**Best Defense**: Igloo, Office Building, Wesley Snipes
**Best vs. Multiple Enemies**: Tornado, Spider, Bridge
**Best for Party Support**: Hot Dog, Bridge, Flower
**Most Fun**: Master Shake (chaos!)

## Mechanics Details

### Turn Order
Transformation happens FIRST (priority -1), before even Warriors' guard actions.

### Passive Effects
Most effects trigger automatically each turn:
- Healing happens at start of turn resolution
- Damage reduction applies when you're attacked
- Damage bonuses apply when you attack
- DoT/AoE effects happen at start of turn

### Stacking
- Only one transformation can be active at a time
- New transformation replaces old one completely
- HP boost from Office Building remains even if you transform again

### Death and Revival
- Transformation persists even if knocked out
- If resurrected, effects continue
- Use `!skills` to check your current form
