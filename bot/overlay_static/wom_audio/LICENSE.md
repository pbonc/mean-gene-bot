# WOM audio sources

The audio files in this directory are edited excerpts of recorded mechanical
sounds. They are not synthesized effects.

## `typewriter.wav`

- Source: `Star-LC-10-printer-01.ogg`
- Recording: Priwo
- Description: Star LC-10 dot-matrix printer from the 1980s
- License: Public domain
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Star-LC-10-printer-01.ogg
- Changes: trimmed to the print animation, converted to mono WAV, normalized,
  and faded at the edit boundaries

## `reel-click.wav`

- Source: `Star-LC-10-printer-01.ogg`
- Recording: Priwo
- Description: Star LC-10 dot-matrix printer from the 1980s
- License: Public domain
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Star-LC-10-printer-01.ogg
- Changes: isolated a carriage-direction impact, trimmed it to a short selector
  transient, reduced high-frequency noise, emphasized its low-mid body, lightly
  compressed it, and faded the edit boundaries

## Other WOM reel sounds

The following are original, generated sound-design assets created specifically
for the Wheel of Misfortune overlay:

- `reel-chatter.wav`
- `reel-lock.wav`

They intentionally favor a stylized 1980s electromechanical identity over
real-world ratchet accuracy. The palette combines split-flap chatter,
pinball-style relay snaps, and a short military-console lock impact.
